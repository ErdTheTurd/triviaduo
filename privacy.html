Privacy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy describes the Trivia Duo iPhone app and this website. Trivia Duo is made by Edward Dunn.

The app works offline

Trivia Duo is a local, two-player game. The question bank is bundled with the app. A match is played on the device you are holding. The app does not need a network connection.

No account, no personal data

The app does not create an account. It does not ask for your name, email address, or other personal information. It does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It does not send gameplay, scores, or questions over the network.

This website

This site is a static marketing site. It does not include a contact form, tracking pixels, or analytics scripts. Fonts are hosted with the site.

Like most websites, your browser may send standard technical requests (such as your IP address) to the host that serves these pages. That is ordinary web hosting behavior.

If you email support@triviaduo.com, we will see whatever you include in that message, and we will use it only to reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
