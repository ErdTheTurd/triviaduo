Privacy

Trivia Duo does not collect personal data.

Trivia Duo is a local, two-player game. It works offline. The question bank is bundled with the app, and a match is played entirely on the device you are holding.

The app does not create accounts, and it does not ask for your name, email address, or other personal information. It does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking. It does not send gameplay, scores, or questions over the network.

This website is a static informational site. If you email support@triviaduo.com, we will see whatever you choose to include in that message, and we will use it only to reply to your question.

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted on this page.
