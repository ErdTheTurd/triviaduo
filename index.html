How a question is scored

The rules are the same on both sides of the phone.

  • Same question

    Each player sees the same prompt and the same four answers, in the same order.

  • First correct tap

    The first player to hit the right answer scores the point. The correct answer is then shown briefly, and the next question starts.

  • A miss locks you out

    A wrong tap locks that player out of the question. The other player can still answer. If both miss, nobody scores.

  • Sixty questions, offline

    The question bank ships in the app: ten facts in each of the six categories. There is no account, no live feed, and no personal data collected.
