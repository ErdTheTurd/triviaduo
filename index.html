Trivia Duo

Head-to-head trivia, face to face on one iPhone.

Two players share one phone and race to the same question. On iPhone Duo, each of you gets your own screen. On a regular iPhone, the screen splits in half and the top half turns to face the other player.

For iPhone Duo and iPhone. Plays fully offline.

How it works

01

Pick a category and a length

Choose all categories or one of six: General, Science, Geography, History, Sports, and Movies & Music. Play 5, 10, or 15 rounds.

02

Sit opposite each other

On iPhone Duo, each player uses their own screen. On a regular iPhone, hold the phone between you: your half faces you, the other half is rotated 180°.

03

Race to the right answer

Both of you see the same question and the same four answers. The first correct tap scores the point. A wrong tap locks that player out of the question.

Features

Made for two

A face-to-face layout that adapts to iPhone Duo and to a regular iPhone, with a scoreboard on both sides and a rematch when the last round ends.

Plays offline

Questions ship with the app. There is no account, no internet connection required, and no live content feed.

A starter question bank

Sixty questions across six categories, written as well-established, unambiguous facts that do not depend on current events.

Clear, focused play

After someone scores — or both players miss — the correct answer is revealed briefly before the next question. Ties are called as ties.
